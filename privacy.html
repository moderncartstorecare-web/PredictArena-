Privacy Policy

At CricPulse, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information in compliance with the Indian Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Information We Collect

We collect your Google profile information (Name, Email, Profile Picture) during sign-up. We also collect transaction data, wallet history, and device information (IP address, browser type) to ensure platform security and prevent fraud.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to operate your account, process deposits/withdrawals via secure payment gateways (such as ZapUPI), provide customer support, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with trusted third-party service providers (like payment gateways and cloud hosting providers) necessary to run the platform.

4. Security & Retention

We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data. Your financial records are retained as long as required by Indian tax and financial regulations. You may request account deletion via the Support Center, subject to settlement of pending dues.
